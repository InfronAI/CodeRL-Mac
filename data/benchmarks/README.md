# Frozen data and attribution

- **APPS**: [Hendrycks et al. / official repository](https://github.com/hendrycks/apps), retrieved from the public [codeparrot/apps mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/codeparrot/apps). The five training problems and their reference programs were already distributed in Salesforce/CodeRL. Six test problems are the first two test rows from the introductory, interview (`all` first rows), and competition configurations, selected before model evaluation. Public example tests are joined by the original problem ID from this repository's `APPS_test_example_tests` release.
- **MBPP**: [Google Research MBPP](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/mbpp), from the exact commit recorded in `provenance.json`. The Google Research repository uses Apache-2.0; preserve its notices and consult the dataset's source for additional terms. `mbpp_all.jsonl` contains 974 tasks for the overlap audit. The execution subset contains test IDs 11–13 and train IDs 601–604.
- **Model**: [Salesforce CodeT5-small](https://huggingface.co/Salesforce/codet5-small), Apache-2.0 according to its model card; the exact revision is pinned in `configs/m1.json`.
- **Authored fixture**: `../fixtures/addition.jsonl` is generated by `scripts/create_fixtures.py`, under this project's license. The question paraphrases share a target algorithm. It is neither an APPS/MBPP subset nor evidence of unseen-task generalization.

`provenance.json` records source URLs, selection rules and hashes. APPS's rows service is not revision-addressable; the checked-in normalized records and retained response hashes establish the specific data used. Reference solutions on test records are kept for provenance/reference checks and are **never exposed to inference or training**.

The APPS content includes programming problems collected from third-party sources; their original rights remain with those sources. This modernization does not relicense upstream datasets or model weights.
